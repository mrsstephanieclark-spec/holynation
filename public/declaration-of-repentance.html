The Declaration of Repentance

The Declaration of Repentance was written by Kim Chadwell while on a fast in March of 2019. It was printed on a scroll and taken to each state in the United States, stopping at every state line to repent, and was read aloud by Kim in front of the United States Capitol.

The Declaration of Repentance was taken to Israel by a friend and was translated there into Hebrew, and has since been translated into Spanish and French as well.
