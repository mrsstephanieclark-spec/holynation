Teacher of the "How"

Kim Chadwell

Known as “America’s Grandma,” Kim Chadwell is founder of Holy Nation and author of the book Dripping In Grace. From orphaned to owned, Kim has turned tragedy to triumph — showing how everyone, with God’s grace, can grow and live in the fullness of happiness.

The Journey

A Vision, Fifty States, and a Message of Repentance

Following instructions given in a vision, Kim traveled to each state in the United States calling for repentance, repenting at each state line. Traveling over 165,000 miles, Kim has sounded the alarm for the urgency of national repentance.

An urgent message begging for the nation to repent was recorded in front of the largest cross in the United States in Branson, Missouri. This message, ironically, was recorded exactly forty days before January 6, 2021.

Kim traveled the nation taking with her the coffin of The Unknown Baby, visiting the many abortion clinics across the country to bring attention to the sanctity of life.

Written Testimony

The Declaration of Repentance & The 95 Theses

2019

The Declaration of Repentance

While on a fast in March of 2019, Kim wrote The Declaration of Repentance. It was printed on a scroll and taken to each state in the nation, stopping at every state line to repent, and was read aloud by Kim in front of the United States Capitol. The Declaration has since traveled to Israel, where it was translated into Hebrew, and is now also available in Spanish and French.

2024

The 95 Theses: The Ninety-Five Sins of the Modern Church

What began as a few notes for speaking turned into an assignment from the Lord. When Kim finally put it all together, it too was ninety-five long — just as Martin Luther’s writings were years ago. The 95 Theses exposes the depth of perversion and counterfeit currently within the church worldwide, and calls the Remnant to immediate change and rebuilding.

Ministry & Family

Teaching God's Word, Season After Season

Kim’s prophetic words have been heard throughout the world. A teacher of God’s Word, Kim hosts two semester-long Bible studies each year, writes a popular blog, and releases regular recordings to her many subscribers.

Kim’s many recordings, along with her prophetic words and warnings, are archived in chronological order in playlists on Rumble.

Kim and her husband have four children and twelve grandchildren.
