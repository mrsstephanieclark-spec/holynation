Founder

Who is Kim Chadwell?

Known as “America’s Grandma,” Kim Chadwell is founder of HOLY NATION and author of the book Dripping In Grace. Following instructions given in a vision, Kim traveled to each state in the United States calling for repentance, repenting at each state line. Traveling thousands of miles, Kim has sounded the alarm for the urgency of national repentance.

An urgent message begging for the nation to repent was recorded in front of the largest cross in the United States, in Branson, Missouri — ironically exactly forty days before January 6, 2021. Kim traveled the nation taking with her the coffin of The Unknown Baby, visiting abortion clinics across the country.

Kim’s prophetic words have been heard throughout the world. A teacher of God’s Word, Kim hosts two semester-long Bible studies each year, writes a popular blog, and releases regular recordings to her many subscribers. Kim and her husband have four children and twelve grandchildren.
