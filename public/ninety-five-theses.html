The 95 Theses

The Ninety-Five Sins of the Modern Church

What started as a few notes for speaking turned into an assignment from the Lord. When finally putting it all together, Kim realized it too was ninety-five long — just as Martin Luther wrote years ago.

Purpose

The purpose of The 95 Theses is to expose, and make aware, the depth of perversion and counterfeit currently within the church worldwide.

Goal

The goal is to alert the Remnant to the need for immediate change and rebuilding.

Is There a Church Without Counterfeit?

Yes. Upon reading The 95 Theses, take out paper and write, by number, the exact opposite of each one listed. Upon writing the opposites of the counterfeit, you will see what the church, designed by Christ, looks like.

How To Be A Part

Soon there will be information with details of how you can participate and help.

Use

The 95 Theses is copyrighted. Permission is given to use for teaching or personal use, as long as you include acknowledgement to Kim Chadwell, Holy Nation.

Please pray as you read The 95 Theses that your heart will be open and hungry for what the Lord is telling the current Church.
